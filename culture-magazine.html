Editorial and publication design

CULTURE Magazine

This two-page magazine spread represents the fusion of traditional fine art and contemporary digital design, bringing together hand-painted portraiture with the precision of modern editorial layout.

The Power of Portraiture: Where Fine Art Meets Digital Design

My goal has always been to make my paintings more than beautiful images. I want my art to serve a purpose—promoting products, telling stories, and connecting with consumers through genuine emotion.

Created for CULTURE Magazine, this two-page spread combines my original hand-painted portraits with digital design. Rather than relying on traditional stock photography, I transformed my paintings into dynamic visual compositions.

This project celebrates art, heritage, and self-expression. It demonstrates how fine art and digital technology can work together to create powerful, memorable advertising.

Technology can generate images, but my artistic vision brings personal expression, emotion, and purpose to every design.

From Pencil to Paint: The Evolution of an Artistic Vision

My creative process begins with a pencil and a brush. This project features my very first oil painting, inspired by my passion for drawing unique figures, shapes, and expressive forms.

What began as an original pencil sketch evolved into an oil painting, a digital illustration, and an exhibition flyer, with the vision of becoming a magazine cover. Each stage demonstrates how I transform traditional fine art into contemporary graphic design.

My creative identity is built on originality. Rather than starting with stock images, I create my own artwork and develop it across different media. From pencil to paint to digital design, every piece tells a story of artistic exploration, imagination, and transformation.
