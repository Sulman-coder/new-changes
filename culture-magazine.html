CULTURE Magazine

From Pencil to Paint: An Artistic Evolution

My creative process begins with original artwork, not stock imagery. This project evolved from a pencil sketch into an oil painting, digital illustration, and exhibition flyer, with a vision for a magazine cover. Each stage reflects my ability to transform traditional fine art into contemporary graphic design while maintaining originality and artistic expression.

This two-page magazine spread represents the fusion of traditional fine art and contemporary digital design, bringing together hand-painted portraiture with the precision of modern editorial layout.
