Book Cover Design

This design originated from a painting I created over the summer. What began as a simple idea evolved into a unique artistic concept. I wanted to experiment with stretching, twisting, and distorting my figures to create a different visual perspective. The result was an expressive and unconventional composition that I later transformed into a book cover design.
