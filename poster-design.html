The Southern Storm | Wrestling Promotional Poster

This assignment challenged me to create an original wrestling character, develop a clever name, and design a promotional poster that captured the excitement of professional wrestling. I created “The Southern Storm,” combining dramatic lightning, bold typography, and powerful imagery to reflect the wrestler’s strength and intensity. Through digital compositing and creative visual storytelling, I transformed the concept into a dynamic, cinematic poster designed to capture attention.

The Unseen Hands / Malikah Reece

This design originated from a painting I created over the summer. What began as a simple idea evolved into a unique artistic concept. I wanted to experiment with stretching, twisting, and distorting my figures to create a different visual perspective. The result was an expressive and unconventional composition that I later transformed into a book cover design.
